How it works

Everyone needs the app; that's all. It works on a campsite, on a train or at the kitchen table.

1

Open a table

One of you taps “Start a game” and picks the game and the house rules.

2

Take a seat

The table shows up on the other phones nearby within a few seconds. Tap it to join.

3

Play

Everyone plays from their own hand. Short of players? Fill the empty seats with bots.

Four classics

Two to seven players, depending on the game.
